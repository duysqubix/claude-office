**See who's done at a glance, and use every slash command and skill right from the chat.**

## New in v1.2.7

**Ready for you:** when someone finishes their turn, they put a hand up and hold a **Done!** card until you've seen it, instead of looking asleep.
- **Easy to spot:**
  - an "N ready" chip next to "needs you";
  - a "Ready for you" group in the roster;
  - markers at the screen's edge when they're out of view;
  - a soft chime and a "<name> finished" toast with their last line, plus **Go** and **Open chat**.
- **Easy to work through:** Q visits anyone who needs you first, then the ready ones. The laptop's Monitor lists them right after.
- **Cleared when you look:** opening their chat, sitting at their computer, seeing them in Monitor or saying something clears it. Walking past doesn't, and neither does a chat left open in a background tab.
- **Switches in Help:** turn the cue or the chime off.

**Slash commands in chat:** type `/` in anyone's chat for the same list Claude Code shows. It has the built-in commands, your own and the project's commands and skills, and your enabled plugins' too, each with its description.
- **Picking:** type to filter, use the arrows, and press Enter or Tab. A name typed in full sends straight away.
- **Everywhere:** it works in the docked chat and in the laptop's Monitor.
