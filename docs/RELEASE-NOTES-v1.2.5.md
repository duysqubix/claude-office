**Watch the whole office from your laptop, and rename anyone.**

## New in v1.2.5

**Monitor on your laptop:** sit at the boss desk, press E and pick **Monitor** at the top of the laptop's screen (Spotify is the other tab).
- **Everyone at a glance:** whoever needs you first, then the busy ones. Each row shows what they're doing and for how long.
- **Pick someone** for a summary: what they're on, what they're doing now, what you asked and what they said, their interns, model and cost.
- **Their chat, right there:** read along, answer their question or talk to them without walking over.
- **Go to them** walks you over, and **Sit at their computer** sits you down there. Esc steps out of a text box first, keeping your draft.

**Rename:** give anyone a name of your own.
- **In their chat:** click the pencil next to their name. Enter keeps the new name and Esc cancels. Leave it empty to give them their usual name back.
- **As they clock in:** the "clocked in" toast has a **Rename** button.
- **The new name sticks:** on their label, in the roster and in the toasts. It stays through restarts, call-backs and moves, and every office on your machine uses it.
- **Never two the same:** a name someone already has is refused, with the reason shown under the box.
